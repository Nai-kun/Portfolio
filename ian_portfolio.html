How I Work

A process built
around people.

Good design is the result of good questions. Each project moves through four stages, and I loop back whenever testing tells me something new.

  1. STEP 01

    Discover

    Understand the users, the goals and the constraints before drawing a single box.

    • Interviews
    • Research
    • Audit
  2. STEP 02

    Define

    Turn findings into clear problems, user flows and an information architecture.

    • Personas
    • User flows
    • IA
  3. STEP 03

    Design

    Wireframe, then build high-fidelity UI with consistent components and type.

    • Wireframes
    • UI kit
    • Hi-fi
  4. STEP 04

    Prototype & Test

    Click-through prototypes to validate flows with real people, then iterate.

    • Prototypes
    • Usability
    • Iteration
