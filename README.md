## Design System & Architecture

- **60 / 30 / 10 Color System:**
  - **60% Matte Black** (`#0E0E10` – `#1B1B1E`) — primary canvas, cards, and non-glossy surface grain.
  - **30% White Frost** (`#F7F8FA` / `#EEF1F5` & frosted glass surfaces) — alternating light editorial sections, primary typography on dark, and frosted UI containers.
  - **10% Navy Blue** (`#1B2D5B` – `#2E4A8F` & `#9DB2E0` accessible tint) — primary CTAs, interactive focus states, badges, and directional accents.
- **Accessibility & Performance:** Semantic landmarks, skip-to-content navigation, keyboard-accessible carousel & native `<dialog>` lightbox/Figma live preview modals, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and optimized external image assets (`~1.8 MB` total vs. `3.9 MB` inline base64).
